# Limitations

- **Only 1 of 3 companies has a usable standalone ad-revenue disclosure.** Nykaa and Eternal are
  documented in the source register with their real scale figures, but their ad-revenue line is
  `INSUFFICIENT_DISCLOSURE` — not estimated, not guessed.
- **Swiggy's food-delivery figure (≥4%) is a disclosed floor, not a point estimate** — the true
  figure could be materially higher; treat the implied INR 360.2cr as a minimum, not an exact number.
- **Swiggy's quick-commerce figure (6-7%) is the company's own forward target, not a current
  achieved rate.** The implied INR 512.3cr is explicitly a target-based projection.
- **Two primary-source PDFs didn't parse this session** (see Source Register) — this benchmark relies
  on financial-press summaries of the underlying filings, not directly re-parsed primary documents.
  A re-attempt with a different extraction method could surface more precise or more current figures.
- **Periods aren't aligned** — Swiggy figures are quarterly (Q4 FY26), Nykaa's is annual (FY26). Any
  chart comparing scale across these two is comparing different time windows, documented explicitly
  on the dashboard.
- **No CPM-based rate card was derived.** None of the three companies discloses impression/traffic
  volume, so a true CPM figure can't be computed from public data — only a monetization-intensity
  metric (ad revenue per rupee of GOV).
- **No number from this project should reach a resume until this file has been re-read and is still
  accurate.**
